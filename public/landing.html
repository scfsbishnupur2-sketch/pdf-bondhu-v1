Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on offline privacy, upgrades & future billing.

Do PDF, Image, Planner, and Expense tools work without internet?

Yes. All core document processing, calendar planning, reminders, expense tracking, calculators, and the encrypted vault run locally on your device using bundled offline libraries and IndexedDB storage. Only optional Cloud Sync and ONEORA AI require an internet connection.

Are live ads or paid subscriptions active right now?

No. ONEORA launches in Free-First Mode with live advertisements and paid billing turned off. All core offline tools remain free forever; optional PLUS and PRO plans will only activate later via verified Google Play Billing or secure web checkout.

Will my existing PDF Bondhu data or vault files be lost when upgrading to ONEORA?

No. ONEORA preserves the exact same Android application ID and local storage keys (automatically mirrored into IndexedDB) so all saved vault entries, recent files, preferences, and records carry forward smoothly.

Are my documents or expense records ever used for advertising?

Never. Private document contents, vault entries, notes, and financial records are processed locally and are never uploaded or shared with advertising networks. All PDF operations — including permanent raster-flattened redaction — execute locally on your device.

How will cancellations, restore purchases, and exports work?

When subscriptions launch on Android, billing and cancellation are handled transparently through Google Play Subscriptions with server-verified entitlement restoration. You can export all local data as JSON/PDF/Excel/CSV or delete synced cloud data anytime from Profile → Privacy & Backup.

Privacy Policy

Local-First Processing: PDFs, Office files, images, notes, habits, expenses, and AES-256 encrypted vault items are processed and stored locally on your device by default.

Optional Cloud Sync: If you choose to sign in with Google, only your Planner and Finance records are stored in your private, owner-restricted Firebase Firestore space. You can delete your cloud data anytime from Profile → Delete Cloud Data.

AI Assistant: Prompts and optional attached excerpts are sent over HTTPS solely to generate your requested reply and are never sold or shared with advertisers.

Terms of Use

Personal & Commercial Productivity: ONEORA is provided as an offline-first productivity suite. Visual signatures placed on PDFs are graphical stamps and not certificate-based digital signatures unless explicitly stated.

Password & Unlock Tools: PDF unlock utilities must only be used on documents you own or are legally authorised to access.

Backups: While ONEORA provides local export and optional Cloud Sync, users remain responsible for maintaining copies of critical legal or financial records.
